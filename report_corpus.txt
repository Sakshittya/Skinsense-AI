Skin health information is educational and cannot replace a clinical examination. A skin lesion can have several causes and similar appearances. Changes in size, shape, color, bleeding, pain, or a wound that does not heal should be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional. Sun protection includes shade, protective clothing, and broad-spectrum sunscreen. Avoid cutting, scraping, or applying harsh chemicals to a skin lesion. A model prediction is an estimate based on its training data and may be incorrect. A dermatologist can examine the skin and decide whether additional tests are needed. Seek urgent care for severe symptoms or rapidly worsening skin problems. Reports should state their limitations and should never claim to confirm a diagnosis. Keep the report factual, cautious, and easy to understand. The model output is not a medical diagnosis. Ask a qualified clinician about individual symptoms and treatment options. The educational report summarizes a model estimate and general safety information. The result should not delay professional assessment of a concerning lesion.
